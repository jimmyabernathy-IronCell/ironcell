About Glutathione

Glutathione is a tripeptide assembled from three amino acids: glutamic acid, cysteine and glycine. It is offered in two strengths, 600mg and 1500mg, each supplied as a single vial.

Keep the vial sealed and recorded in your laboratory inventory until the material is needed. Glutathione is supplied for research use only and is not for human or veterinary consumption. Experimentation should be done by qualified professionals in properly equipped facilities.
