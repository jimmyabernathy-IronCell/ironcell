About KLOW

KLOW is a four-component research blend of BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu and KPV, 80mg in total, supplied in one vial. Each vial holds 80mg.

KPV, GHK-Cu and BPC-157 are each also listed as single-compound vials.

Keep the vial sealed and recorded in your laboratory inventory until the material is needed. KLOW is supplied for research use only and is not for human or veterinary consumption. Experimentation should be done by qualified professionals in properly equipped facilities.
