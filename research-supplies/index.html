About these supplies

What each item is, and how the two kits differ.

Bacteriostatic water: 10ml and Hospira 30ml

Bacteriostatic water is sterile, non-pyrogenic water that contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol. The benzyl alcohol is a bacteriostatic preservative: it inhibits bacterial growth in the vial. Plain sterile water contains no preservative. In the laboratory it is used as a diluent, for example to reconstitute a lyophilized research compound before analysis.

Two sizes are available. The standard vial holds 10ml. The Hospira bacteriostatic water vial holds 30ml, is made by Hospira, and uses the same 0.9% benzyl alcohol preservative.

To work out how much diluent a given vial needs for a target concentration, use the peptide reconstitution calculator. It converts mg to mL and shows the result on a syringe scale.

31 gauge insulin syringes

The syringes are 31 gauge with a 5/16 inch (8mm) needle, sold in packs of 10. The Hospira Starter Kit includes EasyTouch brand 31 gauge syringes.
