What each certificate reports

These ten certificates report four things and nothing else. This is what each line on the report means.

Identity (LC-MS)
Liquid chromatography with mass spectrometry. The laboratory compares the measured mass of the sample with the expected mass of the named compound and records the compound it identified.
Purity (HPLC-UV)
High-performance liquid chromatography with ultraviolet detection. The result is the share of the detected signal that belongs to the identified compound, stated as a percentage.
Net content
The measured mass of material in the tested vial. For blends, the report lists a mass for each component.
Appearance
The physical form and color of the sample as received by the laboratory.
Report number and lot
The report number identifies the laboratory document. The lot identifies the batch the sample came from.
